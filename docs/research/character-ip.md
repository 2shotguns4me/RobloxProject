# Character IP Research: Brainrot, Public Domain, and Open-Licensed Characters

*Researched 2026-10-05. This is not legal advice. It's a practical risk overview for a small team. Claims I couldn't confirm against a primary or reputable secondary source are marked **[unverified]**. Copyright terms below are **US** rules unless stated otherwise. Roblox distributes worldwide, so see the "outside the US" notes.*

---

## Top takeaways for our game

1. **Brainrot characters are not free to use. They're in active litigation.** *Spyder Games LLC v. Mementum Lab SAS* (N.D. Cal., No. 5:25-cv-10248) is still undecided. The Steal a Brainrot devs are asking the court to declare that Mementum owns no valid rights in Tung Tung Tung Sahur and about 22 other brainrot characters. Mementum has counterclaimed for **trademark infringement and unfair competition**. Until that's decided, nobody can tell you these characters are safe ([Dexerto](https://www.dexerto.com/roblox/tung-tung-sahur-is-at-the-center-of-a-bizarre-federal-custody-battle-over-brainrot-characters-3381733/), [NPR/WUNC](https://www.wunc.org/2026-08-21/a-battle-over-italian-brainrot-could-shape-who-owns-ai-art), [Leagle docket order](https://www.leagle.com/decision/infdco20260730b55)).
2. **"AI-generated means public domain" is an argument, not a ruling.** The US Copyright Office says prompts alone don't give enough human control to support copyright, and *Thaler v. Perlmutter* is now final (cert denied March 2, 2026). But Mementum argues that human choices of name, concept, voice and iteration count as authorship, and it also claims trademark rights, which don't depend on copyright at all ([Sidley](https://datamatters.sidley.com/2025/02/07/u-s-copyright-office-issues-report-on-artificial-intelligence-and-copyrightability/), [Copyright Lately](https://copyrightlately.com/thaler-is-dead-ai-copyright-questions/)).
3. **Brainrot mitigation if we still want the vibe:** build *original* absurdist hybrid characters ("object + animal + Italian-ish sing-song name") with our own names, art and voice lines. The style and genre can't be owned. Don't copy specific images, names or the original TikTok audio. A *parody / homage* of a famous one is Medium risk. Using the exact names and looks is High.
4. **Several brainrot originals have content problems on Roblox regardless of IP.** The original Tralalero Tralala audio contains Italian blasphemy against God and Allah. The original Bombardiro Crocodilo audio is about bombing children in Gaza. Roblox bans swearing in any language in uploaded audio and bans content that mocks real-world sensitive events. Never use the original audio.
5. **The public domain gives us a strong roster for free**, mostly at Low risk: myth and folklore (Hercules, Thor, Sun Wukong, Baba Yaga, Krampus, Robin Hood, King Arthur, Paul Bunyan), 19th-century literature (Dracula, Frankenstein's creature as written in the novel, Alice, the Oz cast, Pinocchio, Sherlock Holmes, the Phantom), and silent-era or early cartoons (Steamboat Willie Mickey, Felix 1919-1920s, Count Orlok).
6. **Two traps apply to every PD pick:**
   - **The version trap.** Only traits in the *earliest published* works are free. That means black-and-white, gloveless 1928 Mickey, not modern Mickey. Book Pooh, not Disney's red shirt. Novel Frankenstein, not Universal's flat head and bolts.
   - **The trademark trap.** Companies still hold trademarks on many names (Disney, King Features/Popeye, Fleischer/Betty Boop, ERB/Tarzan, Zorro Productions, Conan Properties, DreamWorks/Felix). Trademark law targets *consumer confusion*, so don't put their names or logos in our game title, thumbnails or merch, and don't imply endorsement ([Duke CSPD on Mickey](https://cspd.law.duke.edu//mickey/)).
7. **Roblox is global, and US public domain isn't world public domain.** Tintin (1929) is PD only in the US and stays protected in the EU until 2054, so skip him. Peter Pan carries a perpetual UK royalty right. Keep "US-only PD" characters out, or treat them as Medium risk.
8. **Coming Jan 1, 2027 (1931 works):** the Universal *Frankenstein* and *Dracula* films (the Karloff and Lugosi looks *as they appear in those films*), Pluto as Mickey's named dog (*The Moose Hunt*), and Dick Tracy. Universal and Tribune/TMS trademarks will still apply, so plan these as reveals, but only *after* the date and with original names.
9. **Open-licensed mascots are a cheap source of guest fighters**, but read the license. Ferris (Rust) is CC0, with no conditions at all. The Go gopher, Godot logo and Pepper&Carrot are CC BY 4.0: credit is required and commercial use is fine. Tux needs credit "if someone asks". Avoid **CC BY-SA and GPL** assets such as most SuperTuxKart models. Their share-alike and "no DRM" terms fit poorly with Roblox's closed platform.
10. **Roblox enforcement:** Roblox removes content when it gets a valid copyright **or trademark** notice. It suspends or terminates repeat or egregious infringers, sometimes after a single strike. You can counter-notify, and content is usually restored within about 14 business days if no lawsuit is filed. Longer term, Roblox's **License Manager** (self-serve since Nov 2025) is the legitimate path if a brainrot rights-holder ever lists characters there ([Roblox DMCA guidelines](https://create.roblox.com/docs/en-us/production/publishing/dmca-guidelines), [Roblox newsroom](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2025/11/roblox-self-serve-ip-licensing)).

## 1. Italian brainrot / AI meme characters

### 1.1 Who claims ownership?

**Tung Tung Tung Sahur.** Indonesian TikToker Fernanda Bagas Indrastata ("Noxa", @noxaasht) generated it on Feb 28, 2025 with DALL-E, from prompts like "draw a pentungan sahur with a face and legs" ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tung_Tung_Tung_Sahur)). NPR reports it took 7 prompts and about 15 minutes ([WUNC/NPR](https://www.wunc.org/2026-08-21/a-battle-over-italian-brainrot-could-shape-who-owns-ai-art)). Paris-based **Mementum Lab** represents Noxa and also names **@ofuscabreno ("Breno")**, creator of Brr Brr Patapim and Frigo Camelo ([roonby](https://roonby.com/2025/09/18/mementum-lab-the-force-behind-the-removal-of-tung-tung-sahur-in-roblox/), [FMT](https://www.freemalaysiatoday.com/category/leisure/2025/08/16/italian-brainrot-the-ai-memes-most-adults-dont-know)).

**Timeline of the Steal a Brainrot dispute:**
- **Sept 2025:** Mementum sends a cease-and-desist and asks for a license. The developer pulls Tung Tung Sahur from the game ([Dexerto](https://www.dexerto.com/roblox/tung-tung-sahur-is-at-the-center-of-a-bizarre-federal-custody-battle-over-brainrot-characters-3381733/), [Beebom](https://beebom.com/roblox-kids-in-shambles-after-steal-a-brainrot-removes-tung-tung-sahur/)).
- **Nov 29, 2025:** The character is restored without a license ([Wikipedia: Steal a Brainrot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steal_a_Brainrot)).
- **Late 2025 / 2026:** Spyder Games and Speedy Simulator Gaming sue in N.D. Cal. (No. 5:25-cv-10248-NW). They seek a declaration that Mementum has no valid copyright in Tung Tung Sahur and about 22 other characters, arguing the characters are AI-generated without human authorship. Mementum counterclaims for **trademark infringement and unfair competition**. It says it has licensed Tung Tung Sahur to *Plants vs Brainrots*, *Pudgy Party*, Fortnite, *Brainrot Fight*, toys and books. The amended complaint was filed July 2026, there's a discovery order dated July 29, 2026, and a motions hearing was set for Sept 16, 2026. **No ruling on the merits was found as of this writing** ([Dexerto](https://www.dexerto.com/roblox/tung-tung-sahur-is-at-the-center-of-a-bizarre-federal-custody-battle-over-brainrot-characters-3381733/), [WUNC/NPR](https://www.wunc.org/2026-08-21/a-battle-over-italian-brainrot-could-shape-who-owns-ai-art), [Leagle](https://www.leagle.com/decision/infdco20260730b55)).
- **Mementum's position:** Noxa's "iterations, experiments, selections, refinements" are human authorship, including under Indonesian Copyright Law No. 28 of 2014 ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tung_Tung_Tung_Sahur)).

**Tralalero Tralala.** The original sound came from @eZburger401, whose account was later banned ([Know Your Meme](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/tralalero-tralala)). Italian company **Officina Comunicazione S.R.L.** filed "TRALALERO TRALALA" word-mark applications in several countries in late 2025. Commentators question whether the mark is distinctive and whether the filings were made in good faith ([PONS IP](https://ponsip.com/en/ip-news/colombia-news/italian-brainrot-as-a-distinctive-sign-can-it-be-registered-as-a-trademark/)). Status, jurisdictions and classes are **[unverified]**. Check USPTO search (tmsearch.uspto.gov) and EUIPO eSearch before launch.

**Fortnite.** Epic released official Tung Tung Tung Sahur and Ballerina Cappuccina skins on Apr 3, 2026 ([Beebom](https://beebom.com/fortnite-tung-tung-tung-sahur-ballerina-cappuccina-skin/amp/), [Daily Dot](https://dailydot.com/ballerina-cappuccina-tung-tung-tung-sahur-fortnite)). That's a sign big companies *pay* for these characters. Who exactly licensed Ballerina Cappuccina is **[unverified]**.

**Other suits.** Spyder Games also sued the Fortnite clone *Stealing Brainrots* in Oct 2025 over game artwork and design, relying on its own US copyright registration (PA0002544969) ([Aftermath](https://aftermath.site/brainrot-roblox-court/)). Lesson: even if the characters are free, **another game's specific 3D models and art are not**. Never copy models or assets from Steal a Brainrot or other brainrot games.

### 1.2 Is AI-generated character art copyrightable?

- **US Copyright Office, Report Part 2 (Jan 29, 2025):** "prompts alone do not provide sufficient human control". Revised or repeated prompts don't change that. Human-authored expression, creative selection and arrangement, and meaningful modifications *can* be protected ([Sidley](https://datamatters.sidley.com/2025/02/07/u-s-copyright-office-issues-report-on-artificial-intelligence-and-copyrightability/), [Mayer Brown](https://www.mayerbrown.com/en/insights/publications/2025/02/human-input-necessary-for-copyrightability-of-works-created-with-artificial-intelligence)).
- ***Thaler v. Perlmutter*:** The D.C. Circuit (March 2025) affirmed that copyright requires human authorship. The Supreme Court **denied cert on March 2, 2026**. The court also said AI-*assisted* works aren't automatically excluded. *Allen v. Perlmutter* (hundreds of iterative Midjourney prompts) is testing that line ([Copyright Lately](https://copyrightlately.com/thaler-is-dead-ai-copyright-questions/)).
- **What it means for us:**
  - A purely prompted brainrot image probably has thin or no US copyright. But names, voice performances, lore text, music and later human-made edits may be protected.
  - **Trademark is a separate track.** If a name is used in commerce as a brand, AI origin doesn't matter.
  - Other countries (Indonesia, Italy, France) may treat it differently.
  - **Practical risk: High** for copying named characters, regardless of how the US case ends.
- **For our own art:** if we use AI tools, have humans substantially design, model and modify the result, and keep records of the process. Otherwise we may be unable to protect our *own* fighters from clones.

### 1.3 Mitigation playbook for brainrot-style content

1. **Make original characters in the genre.** Hybrids like "object + animal + Italian-ish name" are an unprotectable style or idea. Examples: an espresso-machine rhino, a pizza-cutter flamingo. Give them new names that don't echo existing ones. "Tralalero-ish" is risky; "Tralalina" is too close.
2. **No original audio.** Don't use the TikTok voiceover clips or the TTS lines. Write our own lines and record them with our own voice or properly licensed TTS.
3. **Don't trace or image-match any specific AI image.** Build our own 3D models.
4. **Keep brainrot names out of the game title, thumbnail, description and tags.** That avoids trademark-style "source confusion" and Roblox trademark notices.
5. **If we ever want the real ones, license them** through Mementum (Tung Tung Sahur and Breno's characters) or through Roblox License Manager if they list there.
6. **Build to swap.** Make every fighter skin hot-swappable so a takedown means a reskin, not a rebuild. Steal a Brainrot survived because the character was removable.

## 2. Public domain characters

### 2.1 The rule (US)

- **Works published 1930 and earlier entered the US public domain on Jan 1, 2026.** That's the 95-year term for pre-1978 works that were published and renewed.
- **Sound recordings** from 1925 also entered on that date ([Duke CSPD 2026](https://cspd.law.duke.edu//publicdomainday/2026/), [LLRX](https://www.llrx.com/2025/12/january-1-2026-is-public-domain-day-works-from-1930-are-open-to-all/)).
- **Next date:** Jan 1, 2027 brings in 1931 works.
- Some works fell into PD earlier because their copyright was never renewed (e.g. Buck Rogers' 1928 novella, some Conan stories). Proving non-renewal requires research, so treat it as **[verify per work]**.

### 2.2 The "later version" trap

Copyright in a character lasts in waves. When a work expires, only the traits *first shown in that work* become free. Later additions stay protected until their own works expire. This is the *Klinger v. Conan Doyle Estate* principle ([Copyright Lately 2026](https://copyrightlately.com/public-domain-2026/)).

- **Mickey:** gloves (1929), color, and the *Fantasia* (1940) look are still protected. Generic traits like a "high voice" or being "adorable" are free ([Duke CSPD](https://cspd.law.duke.edu//mickey/)).
- **Betty Boop:** the 1930 *Dizzy Dishes* version is a half-poodle with floppy dog ears. Her human look came later. Fleischer Studios says only "a precursor" became free ([Copyright Lately](https://copyrightlately.com/public-domain-2026/)).
- **Popeye:** only the Jan 1929 *Thimble Theatre* strip version is free, not the 1933+ animated version ([Smithsonian](https://www.smithsonianmag.com/smart-news/happy-public-domain-day-popeye-rhapsody-in-blue-the-sound-and-the-fury-and-thousands-of-other-captivating-creations-are-finally-free-for-everyone-to-use-180985744/)).
- **Pluto:** 1930 gives only the bloodhound and "Rover". The named Pluto as Mickey's dog is 1931 (*The Moose Hunt*), so Jan 1, 2027 ([Copyright Lately](https://copyrightlately.com/public-domain-2026/)).

**Practical rule:** design from the *original* source images, document which source you used, and give each design our own twist.

### 2.3 The trademark trap

Trademarks never expire while they're in use. They stop confusion about *who makes or endorses a product*. They don't block making a new creative work with a PD character. Duke CSPD: "trademark law cannot be used to block the freedoms that the expiration of copyright allows" ([Duke CSPD](https://cspd.law.duke.edu//mickey/)).

In practice, though, rights-holders send cease-and-desist letters and Roblox notices anyway. Roblox removes content on a valid **trademark** notice, not just copyright ones ([Roblox DMCA guidelines](https://create.roblox.com/docs/en-us/production/publishing/dmca-guidelines)). Examples of active enforcers:
- ERB Inc. sued Dynamite over Tarzan and John Carter comics based on PD novels ([Techdirt](https://www.techdirt.com/2012/03/09/edgar-rice-burroughs-inc-using-trademark-law-to-prevent-use-public-domain-stories/), [Comics Beat](https://www.comicsbeat.com/erb-inc-sues-dynamite-and-company-over-tarzan-and-john-carter)).
- Zorro Productions Inc. lost key rulings in *Cabell v. Zorro Productions* and had its EU mark cancelled, but still enforces ([Loeb & Loeb](https://www.loeb.com/en/insights/publications/2018/05/cabell-v-zorro-productions-inc), [Techdirt](https://www.techdirt.com/2015/07/13/europe-frees-zorro-trademark-restrictions/)).
- Conan Properties International holds the "Conan the Barbarian" mark ([Fanlore summary](https://fanlore.org/wiki/Conan_the_Barbarian)).
- The Dille Family Trust uses trademarks to keep licensing Buck Rogers ([Boing Boing](https://boingboing.net/2015/10/16/buck-rogers-and-the-copyright.html)).
- DreamWorks/NBCU owns the Felix the Cat trademark ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Felix_the_Cat)).
- Universal reportedly sent a C&D over the five-element Frankenstein look: bolts, green skin, big forehead, scar, flat top ([Cord Cutters News](https://cordcuttersnews.com/universal-horror-classics-frankenstein-and-dracula-are-set-to-enter-public-domain-soon/), [Plagiarism Today](https://www.plagiarismtoday.com/2011/10/24/how-universal-re-copyrighted-frankensteins-monster/)).

**Mitigations:**
- Use variant or descriptive names ("Steamboat Mouse", "The Masked Fox of California", "Jungle Lord") instead of trademarked names in titles, thumbnails, game passes and merch.
- Never use their logos or title lettering.
- Add a disclaimer: "not affiliated with or endorsed by…".
- Avoid UGC merch or avatar items of trademarked characters entirely.

### 2.4 Outside the US

Roblox is global, and most countries use life of the author + 70 years.
- **Tintin:** Hergé died in 1983, so EU protection runs to 2054 even though the 1929 US work is PD ([Smithsonian/Mental Floss coverage](https://www.mentalfloss.com/culture/media/works-entering-the-public-domain-in-2025)). **Avoid.**
- **Peter Pan:** UK CDPA 1988 Schedule 6 gives Great Ormond Street Hospital a perpetual royalty on performance, publication or communication of the play *and adaptations* in the UK ([GOSH](https://www.gosh.org/about-us/peter-pan/copyright), [SpicyIP](https://spicyip.com/2016/02/spicyip-trivia-the-royalties-that-never-grow-up.html)). Whether a fighting-game appearance triggers it is unclear, so it's Medium risk. Skip him or contact GOSH.
- **US-origin works in the EU:** many EU countries apply the "rule of the shorter term", so US-PD works are often PD there too. **Germany has an 1892 US–German treaty that may override this** **[unverified, so get advice]**. This matters for 1928 Mickey (Disney and Iwerks died 1966 and 1971).
- **Ancient myth, folklore and 19th-century novels are PD everywhere.** That's why they're our safest picks.

### 2.5 Character-by-character notes

- **Steamboat Willie Mickey and Minnie (1928, PD 2024):** use the black-and-white, no-gloves look ([Duke CSPD 2024](https://cspd.law.duke.edu//publicdomainday/2024/)). The *Steamboat Willie* film's later added music may still be protected. No "Mickey" in titles. **Medium.**
- **Winnie-the-Pooh (1926 book, PD 2022) and Tigger (1928, PD 2024):** use Shepard's illustrations, no red shirt, no Disney voice. **Medium** (Disney attention).
- **Popeye (PD 2025):** 1929 strip version. King Features trademark. Spinach-powered invincibility is in the 1929 strip **[unverified exact debut of the spinach trait; early strips gave power via the Whiffle Hen]**. **Medium.**
- **Betty Boop (PD 2026):** only the dog-eared *Dizzy Dishes* version. Fleischer actively licenses and disputes. **Medium–High.** Probably skip.
- **Sherlock Holmes (fully PD 2023):** the Doyle estate's Enola Holmes suit was settled and the last stories expired ([Fox 7 / AP](https://www.fox7austin.com/news/2023-public-domain-debuts-sherlock-holmes)). **Low.**
- **Dracula (1897), Count Orlok (1922), Frankenstein (1818), Phantom (1910 / 1925 film):** **Low** if we use our own designs. Frankenstein's creature should *not* combine the Universal elements before 1/1/2027, and even after that, copy only the 1931 film look, not *Bride of Frankenstein* (1935).
- **Oz cast (1900 book):** use silver shoes, not ruby slippers. Avoid MGM's green Witch and the Bert Lahr lion look. **Low.**
- **Alice (1865), Pinocchio (1883), Robin Hood, King Arthur, myths, Sun Wukong, Paul Bunyan, fairy tales, Krampus, Baba Yaga, cryptids:** **Low.** Avoid copying Disney, Marvel or *Black Myth* designs. Treat living religions respectfully, since Norse and Greek gods are fine but deities of active faiths can trigger Roblox's religion rules.
- **Zorro:** the 1919 novel and 1920 film are PD, and courts sided against ZPI on key claims, but ZPI still enforces. **Medium–High.** Use a renamed masked swordsman.
- **Tarzan / John Carter:** ERB Inc. is very active. **High.** Use a renamed "jungle lord" archetype.
- **Conan:** CPI trademark, and copyright status per story is murky. **High.** Use a generic barbarian.
- **Buck Rogers:** trademark disputes. **Medium–High.**
- **Felix (1919–1920s cartoons):** design PD, but DreamWorks owns the name trademark. **Medium.**

### 2.6 Coming Jan 1, 2027 (1931 works)

- Universal's ***Frankenstein*** (Karloff/Pierce makeup as seen in the 1931 film) and ***Dracula*** (Lugosi), plus the Spanish-language *Drácula*. Lugosi and Karloff estates have publicity rights in their *likeness*, Universal holds trademarks, and later films stay protected ([Cord Cutters News](https://cordcuttersnews.com/universal-horror-classics-frankenstein-and-dracula-are-set-to-enter-public-domain-soon/)).
- **Pluto** as Mickey's named dog, from *The Moose Hunt* (Apr 30, 1931) ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Moose_Hunt), [Copyright Lately](https://copyrightlately.com/public-domain-2026/)).
- **Dick Tracy** (strip from Oct 1931). Tribune Content Agency still holds trademarks **[unverified current owner]**.
- Also 1931: Chaplin's *City Lights* and Lang's *M* ([Cord Cutters News](https://cordcuttersnews.com/universal-horror-classics-frankenstein-and-dracula-are-set-to-enter-public-domain-soon/)).
- **Looking further ahead:** Conan's first story (Dec 1932) and Goofy's precursor "Dippy Dawg" (1932) arrive in 2028 **[unverified dates]**.

**Why CC BY-SA and GPL are Medium on Roblox:**
- **Share-alike:** our derivative model would also have to be CC BY-SA.

## 4. Roblox-specific rules

- **Terms of Use and Community Standards:**
  - "We expect you to respect the intellectual property rights of others, and will remove infringing content upon receiving a valid copyright or trademark takedown request" ([Community Standards](https://about.roblox.com/community-standards)).
  - Creators are responsible for having rights to what they upload: "the responsibility is yours" ([Roblox DevForum DMCA FAQ](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/answers-to-your-recent-questions/256614)).
  - The Terms of Use page itself returned 403 to automated fetch. Read it directly at en.help.roblox.com.
- **DMCA process:**
  - Rights-holders file at copyright_agent@roblox.com, trademark_agent@roblox.com, or via the IP Platform. Roblox "act[s] quickly to take down" content and notifies the creator.
  - Creators can counter-notify under penalty of perjury. Content is typically restored within about 14 business days unless the claimant sues.
  - Roblox "suspends or terminates accounts" of users who "repeatedly or egregiously infringe" ([DMCA guidelines](https://create.roblox.com/docs/en-us/production/publishing/dmca-guidelines)).
  - Strikes come only from rights-holder notices, not automated filters. Some expire and some are permanent. One strike can mean termination in egregious cases ([DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/answers-to-your-recent-questions/256614)).
- **How often games get taken down:** I couldn't find public counts of DMCA'd experiences. Roblox's EU DSA transparency report covers notices in aggregate ([summary](https://kieranmaynard.com/blog/roblox-dsa-2025.html)).
  - Famous cases: *Pokémon Brick Bronze* was removed after a Nintendo notice (2017–18) ([Sportskeeda](https://sportskeeda.com/roblox-news/5-roblox-games-removed-platform)).
  - Steal a Brainrot was *not* taken down, but pulled a character under C&D pressure.
  - Takedowns are common for well-known franchises (Nintendo, Disney, Toho, etc.) **[general impression; unverified rate]**.
- **License Manager (licensing program):**
  - Launched July 2025 with Lionsgate, Netflix, SEGA and Kodansha. Self-serve since Nov 2025, with Mattel IPs and more added.
  - IP holders set the revenue share (typically **10–25%**). Some licenses have DAU minimums: SEGA's *Like a Dragon* gives a 50% split but requires 1,000 DAU. Netflix leaves creators 15% with no minimum.
  - IP holders can "proactively scan for IP usage", so unlicensed use of listed IP is *more* detectable.
  - Over 1,500 license requests by Nov 2025 ([Roblox July 2025](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2025/07/roblox-launches-new-licensing-platform-for-experiences), [Roblox Nov 2025](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2025/11/roblox-self-serve-ip-licensing), [Kidscreen](https://kidscreen.com/?p=224295)).
  - **Future route:** if Mementum or another brainrot rights-holder registers characters there, we can apply. Creator eligibility rules are at create.roblox.com/docs/ip-licensing.
- **Content rules relevant to a fighter:** cartoon violence is fine at Minimal/Mild. "Sensitive issues" (religious, political) must be declared. Strong language is Restricted (18+) and swearing in uploaded audio is banned ([maturity docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/content-maturity)).

## 5. Recommended roster strategy

1. **Core roster (Low risk):** 6–8 original brainrot-style characters plus myth/folklore/classic-lit fighters. Suggested: Sun Wukong, Hercules, Baba Yaga, Krampus, Count Orlok-inspired vampire, the Phantom, Paul Bunyan, Robin Hood, Alice, Oz Tin Man (book), Sherlock, Pinocchio, Ferris/Pepper as guests.
2. **Spice (Medium):** "Steamboat"-style 1928 mouse under an original name, 1929-strip sailor, Felix-style 1920s cat. Rename them, follow the version rules, and keep them out of the thumbnail.
3. **2027 drop:** classic-monster DLC on or after Jan 1, 2027, with original names and only 1931-film traits.
4. **Avoid (High):** named brainrot originals and their audio, Tintin, Tarzan/John Carter, Conan, Betty Boop, anything from MGM/Disney/Universal *later* versions.
5. **Process:**
   - Keep a `docs/characters/` provenance note per fighter: source work, year, the reference image used, and what we changed.
   - Keep skins swappable.
   - Have a lawyer review before any paid marketing or merch.
